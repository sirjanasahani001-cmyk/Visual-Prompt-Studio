Prompt 1.
HIstorical theme: Art as divine power
Historical period: ANcient Egypt
Artwork inspiration: Khafre with The Falcon God Horus

Visual Prompt:
Create a statue carved of dark, polished stone portraying a seated Pharaoh from Ancient Egypt. The figure is positioned facing the viewer, the Pharaoh sitting on his throne with a stiff and formal posture, large shoulder line, and overall figure symmetrical and balanced. The shape is closed, completely polished and idealized rather than depicting actual human form characterizing the notion of strength and perfection. A falcon deity spreading its wings behind the Pharaoh’s head in a protective gesture. The feathers are finely detailed. Along the sides of the throne, hieroglyphics are engraved. The statue seems motionless and eternal without any trace of movement, highlighting stability and continuity. The piece was commissioned to represent the Pharaoh as a god-king whose powers were bestowed upon him by the deities. The piece symbolizes the concept of ma’at (order and balance) and was intended to survive eternally, thereby displaying the Pharaoh’s command in the world of the living as well as the ‍ ‌‍ ‍‌afterlife.


Prompt 2. 
Historical theme: Cathedrals as spiritual hubs for travelers and pilgrims
Historical period: Gothic
Artwork inspiration: Chartres Cathedral

Visual Prompot: 
Imagine a towering Gothic cathedral rising above a medieval town, It’s exterior design creating strong vertical lines attracts the viewers eyes upward toward the sky. The structure is consisted of different pointed arches, flying buttresses, and a forest of slender columns which creates sense of height light and upward movement. The entrance feature statues of saints and biblical figures grouped in a repetitive patterns. Inside of that cathedral a huge stained glass window light the area with colors of red, blue, and gold, creating an extraordinary atmosphere. The interior is spacious and orderly, including ribbed vaults and a long center aisle that directs attention to the sacrifice. This cathedral was built as place of worships and as a center of pilgrims traveling from various places. Its large scale, detailed designs and uses of lights shows how Gothic period was influenced by the religious values. This also indicates how economically and socially important it was for the churches to attract pilgrims.
